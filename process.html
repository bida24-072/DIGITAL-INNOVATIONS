How We Work

A process that
actually works.

No vague timelines. No hidden surprises. Just clear stages, clear deliverables, and results you can see.

01

Discovery

We sit down, ask the hard questions, and figure out what success actually looks like for your business — before we write a single line of code.

02

Strategy

We map the scope, recommend the right approach, and give you a clear timeline and price. No guesswork. No surprises later.

03

Design

Wireframes, prototypes, and iterating until the experience feels right — all before we build. You approve the design, then we build.

04

Build

Clean, scalable code. Fast, tested, mobile-first. We ship on the schedule we agreed to — not on "African time."

05

Launch

We hand over a live product, walk you through everything, and stay on to make sure it works in the real world.

06

Support

After launch, we stick around. Updates, fixes, new features — we grow with your business, not just build once.
